### 6. FEN edge behaviors
- EXPECTED: consecutive digits rejected (SF-1); whitespace-padded input stored trimmed (SF-2); impossible-but-parseable accepted (AC3); malformed → TypeError; structural → RangeError.
- OBSERVED: `RNBQK11R` rank → `isFen` false, `requireValidFen` RangeError (matches vendored `validate_fen` error 8 "consecutive numbers", verified independently). `'  <FEN>\t'` stored as canonical FEN by both FEN factories. 16-queen no-king FEN accepted and stored verbatim; 3-king FEN accepted and stored verbatim. Non-strings/`'   '` → TypeError; 7-rank FEN and fullmove-0 FEN → RangeError.
- **Oracle-limitation finding (characterized, not a defect):** the vendored `validate_fen` reports the 3-king FEN as "valid" (no king-count check in this version), yet `new Chess(threeKingFen).fen()` silently drops the extra king. The chess.js oracle therefore corrupts some impossible positions. This is precisely why contract §2.2's structural-only validator (which preserves such FENs verbatim) is the honest design, and why AC9-style replay proofs are only meaningful on legal positions. No module change needed; noted for §7.2 groundwork.
- Verdict: PASS (with the noted oracle limitation).
