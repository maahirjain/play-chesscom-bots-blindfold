## Repair SF-1 — game_ended schema reconciliation (adversarial review, 2026-10-06)

**Defect:** 3.5 shipped a second, incompatible `game_ended` schema
(`{termination, source, speechLogicVersion}`) with its own 10-member
`TERMINATIONS` vocabulary and a same-named `requireValidGameEndedPayload`
that silently overwrote 1.4's validator on the merged namespace
(manifest load order: game_records.js before chess_utils.js).
Reproduced empirically by the reviewer: 1.4-shaped payloads were
rejected, 3.5-shaped accepted. It also forked the termination vocabulary
(renamed members) and contradicted 1.4's consumer contract (multiple
`game_ended` permitted, latest-wins vs 3.5's first-wins).

**Repair (reconcile, don't fork):**
- chess_utils.js: removed the duplicate `GAME_ENDED_EVENT_TYPE`
  assignment, the `TERMINATIONS` vocabulary, and 3.5's
  `requireValidGameEndedPayload` (the shadowing export). 1.4's
  game_records.js is untouched.
- The 3.5 recorder binds 1.4's `createGameEndedPayload` via
  `sharedBS()` (3.1 precedent) and builds every `game_ended` in 1.4's
  shape `{result, terminationReason, evidenceSource, observedText}`.
- `chessRulesTermination` now returns 1.4 `TERMINATION_REASONS` members
  (`draw_threefold`, `draw_insufficient_material`, `draw_fifty_move`);
  new `chessRulesResult` derives the PGN result (`1-0`/`0-1`/`1/2-1/2`),
  mirroring `getResultAnnouncement`'s winner logic. Both are covered by
  3.4.4's `SPEECH_LOGIC_VERSION` bump rule (same derivation conditions).
  `speechLogicVersion` is gone from the payload (not in 1.4's schema).
- API: `recordGameEnded(source, input, terminalMoveEventId)` (source =
  recorder-internal dedup key, never persisted);
  `recordChessRulesEnded(game, terminalMoveEventId)` (null when not
  over; honest unknown record `{result:'*', terminationReason:null}`
  for unrecognized terminal states); `recordDialogEnded(input, id)`
  (§7, unwired — forces `evidenceSource:'observed'`);
  `recordStopTermination(reason, result)` (reason ∈
  TERMINATION_REASONS|null, result default `'*'`).
- Dedup resolved per 1.4's contract: per-source idempotency (each
  source ≤ 1 per game) with multiple `game_ended` permitted across
  sources; a manual Stop completion supersedes an earlier
  auto-detection (consumers take latest by occurrence time).
- `GAME_END_SOURCES` (`chess_rules`, `chesscom_dialog`, `stop`) is now
  documented as a recorder-internal dedup vocabulary, never persisted.
