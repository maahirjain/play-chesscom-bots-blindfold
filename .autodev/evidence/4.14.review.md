## Generation scoping (focus area 4)

`attachStream` calls `detachStream(kind)` first (clearing health),
then initializes a fresh `{lastRecorderError: null,
lastDiscontinuity: null}` at the end. `emitRestartDiscontinuities`
runs *after* the attaches in `recorder.js` (line 1283 vs 1275), so a
retained `'restart'` is never clobbered by the fresh generation.
No stale-health leak across generations; document restart empties
the mirror by construction (contract §7).

## NOTEs (informational; none fails an AC)

1. `nowUtcIso` is accepted by `createStreamStatus` but never used —
   the per-stream shape has no `queriedAtUtc` field (it lives on the
   channel envelope, stamped by `recorder.js`). Dead-but-harmless;
   minor cleanliness only.
2. `chunk: null` is ambiguous between "chunker not yet polled" and
   "chunker failed to start" (4.8's auto-start is best-effort). The
   latter requires a wiring defect, not a runtime condition, and the
   status honestly reports unknown (null) rather than fabricating.
   A §5 readiness gate keyed on lifecycle alone could not
   distinguish it — worth §5 knowing, not a 4.14 defect.
3. Late-chunk-after-finalize (4.13's documented design: "finalization
   closes the segment, not the store"): status may read `'finalized'`
   while a late chunk exists in the store. Honest per the segment's
   state; §6's export-time concern, not 4.14's.
4. `unfinalizedSegments` counts superseded generations too
   (kind+session scoped, per contract §1) — honest, and the right
   signal for 4.13's crash-recovery path.
5. V2 was verified structurally (harness exists, assertions match
   claims); the independent Chrome re-run belongs to the behavioral
   verifier.
6. The `getStreamStatusReader` thunks lazily construct
   `getStreamStarter()`/`getChunkWriter()` inside the read path
   (builder's open item). Thrown errors degrade to null via
   `safeRead`; acceptable for a query path and avoids lifecycle
   coupling. No defect.
