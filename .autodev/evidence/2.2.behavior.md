## NOTE-1 (not a defect): raw DOMException on missing-keyPath put

`withStore` catches the *synchronous* `DataError` thrown by `store.put()`
when the record lacks its keyPath and rejects with the raw `DOMException`
instead of the `wrapIDBError` plain `Error` the contract specifies for
"IDB request/transaction failures". Analysis:

- The failure is **loud and immediate** (rejects, never hangs or silently
  writes). No data-loss or corruption risk.
- It only triggers on a caller bug (record missing its key) — and record
  validation is explicitly the **2.4 writer's** job, which will never feed
  keyless records here.
- Wrapping it would be a one-line change in `withStore`'s catch path, but
  the contract's error table arguably covers async request failures, not
  sync key-extraction throws.

**Recommendation for the 2.4 planner:** either wrap sync `store.*` throws
in `wrapIDBError` for full convention uniformity, or document that
keyless-record puts surface the platform `DataError`. Not a 2.2 blocker.

## Carried-forward observation (from the 2.2 review, confirmed)

`withStore` resolves on **request success**, not `transaction.oncomplete`.
For 2.2's single-op API this is benign (Chrome commits barring commit-time
quota failure), but the **2.4 contract should explicitly require
commit-awaiting semantics** for the transactional writer — a commit-time
abort after a resolved `put` would be a silent durability lie.
