## 1. What "verify" means

PLAN.md §7.14 requires the README to document four areas for the human
operator (Maahir):

1. **Setup:** how to load the extension (chrome://extensions, developer mode,
   load unpacked), grant permissions (microphone, camera, screen capture),
   and configure Chess.com (bot game page, recommended settings — already in
   README, keep).
2. **Recording controls:** the in-page Start/Stop cluster — what each control
   does (category/training fields, detected conditions, readiness badge,
   moment marker, Download button), what the lights mean, and what the
   readiness badge states mean (waiting → ready; never gates Stop).
3. **Export instructions:** how to download the ZIP (Download button after
   Stop), where it goes (browser download manager), extracting into the
   experiment directory (integrate EXPORT.md's content — 6.8 deferred this
   to 7.14), the bundle layout (`metadata.json`, `events.jsonl`,
   `media-sync.json`, numbered media files), and re-export after interruption.
4. **Verified coverage limitations:** an honest list of what was verified at
   each level (V1/V2 in CI/cloud, V3/V4 on device) and what remains
   device-only — e.g. "tab-audio capture verified on [device]; physical
   mute/unplug behavior characterized on [device]". This section must be
   updatable after the owner runs the V3/V4 checks (7.8, 7.9, 7.12, 7.13).
