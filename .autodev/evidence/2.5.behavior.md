## Check 1 — Never-settling transport: timeout, queue retention, retry cadence, recovery (Node)

**Setup:** `createSender({transport: never-settling, sendTimeoutMs: 200, retryIntervalMs: 150})`;
emit 2 moves for one session (anchor + 2 moves = 3 envelopes queued).

| 1.1 | 3 envelopes queued | `pendingCount() === 3` | PASS |

| 1.4 | Retry timer re-attempts (≥3 attempts over 750 ms) | 4 attempts observed | PASS |
| 1.5 | Every attempt targets the head (stop-and-wait) | all attempt eventIds equal the anchor's id | PASS |
| 1.6 | `retryScheduled === true` just after a timeout | `true` sampled post-timeout, pre-timer-fire | PASS |

**Recovery:** transport flipped to immediate correct acks; polled for drain.

| # | Expected | Observed | Result |
|---|----------|----------|--------|
| 1.7 | Queue drains once transport recovers | `pendingCount() === 0` within ~1 s of recovery | PASS |
| 1.8 | `retryScheduled === false`, `lastError === null` after drain | both confirmed | PASS |
| 1.9 | Each queued event sent at least once, in order | 3 distinct eventIds, anchor first | PASS |

**Harness self-corrections (module was right):** my first draft sampled
`retryScheduled` at an arbitrary instant (false — timer already consumed by the
in-flight attempt) and expected `flush()` to wait out a dead transport (it
correctly joins the in-flight attempt, which must time out first). Both
expectations were fixed; the module behaved per contract in each case.

## Check 2 — Late-ack safety: no double-dequeue, no corruption (Node)

**Setup:** transport resolves `{ok:true, eventId}` 300 ms after each call;
`sendTimeoutMs: 200`, `retryIntervalMs: 150`. One emit (anchor + 1 move).

| # | Expected | Observed | Result |
|---|----------|----------|--------|
| 2.1 | Attempt 1 times out at 200 ms; late ack at 300 ms discarded | send sequence `[anchor, anchor, move]` | PASS |
| 2.2 | No double-dequeue: anchor still head at retry time | 3 sends for 2 events; anchor sent twice | PASS |
| 2.3 | Queue drains exactly once per event | `pendingCount() === 0`; `retryScheduled === false`; `lastError === null` | PASS |

The discarded late ack never shifted the queue — the race loser is fully
ignored (the `.then` handler never runs for it), so a late ack cannot be
misattributed to the new head. `isPositiveAck`'s eventId match is a second
layer that was never even reached by the stale response.

## Check 3 — Uniform retry on `{ok:false}`: head-of-line blocking (Node)

**Setup:** transport always resolves `{ok:false, eventId: <head>, error: 'simulated'}`
after 20 ms; `sendTimeoutMs: 500`, `retryIntervalMs: 150`. Two emits (3 queued).

| # | Expected | Observed | Result |
|---|----------|----------|--------|
| 3.1 | Head retried repeatedly (uniform retry, no classification) | ≥4 attempts over 800 ms | PASS |
| 3.2 | Every attempt targets the head; events behind stay queued in order | all attempt ids equal anchor id; `pendingCount() === 3` | PASS |
| 3.3 | `lastError` carries the writer's string; retry stays scheduled | `lastError === 'simulated'`; `retryScheduled === true` | PASS |

This pins the contracted head-of-line blocking: a deterministically-rejected
head wedges the queue loudly (2.8 can see `pendingCount`/`lastError`/
`retryScheduled`) rather than being silently skipped or dropped.

## Check 6 — Real content-script world: flaky listener, timer-driven recovery (browser)

**Setup:** /tmp extension copy (5 product files byte-identical); real SW;
flaky `onMessage` listener ignores the first 3 sends (returns `false`),
timestamps every received message, then delegates to the real writer.
Driver creates `createSender({sendTimeoutMs: 500, retryIntervalMs: 500})`,
emits 3 moves, and **never calls `flush()` in its poll loop** — any recovery
is the sender's own retry timer.

| # | Expected | Observed | Result |
|---|----------|----------|--------|
| 6.1 | Driver completes; queue drains via timer alone | `phase: 'done'`, `pendingCount: 0` | PASS |
| 6.2 | SW saw ≥7 attempts (3 ignored + 4 delivered) | exactly 7 | PASS |
| 6.3 | Inter-attempt gaps match the 500 ms retry interval | gaps `[514, 501, 502]` ms | PASS |
| 6.4 | 4 records durable (anchor + 3 moves), gapless `appendSeq` 0–3, no dup eventIds | confirmed via `DB.getAll('events')` in the SW | PASS |

The ~500 ms gaps (6.3) are the decisive evidence: recovery cadence equals the
injected retry interval, proving the retry *timer* — not `emit()` kicks —
drove the redelivery.
