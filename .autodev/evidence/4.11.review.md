## Focus-area findings

1. **PLAN extension honesty.** PLAN.md §4.11 line 175 reads: "Add a
   visible/audible synchronization marker at session start and
   stop and save its source timestamps." The planner's "start AND
   stop" framing is faithful to PLAN, not an extension. The
   decomposition (4.11 builds the mechanism + wires START + defines
   the exact `emitStopMarker()` seam; 4.13 wires STOP) is
   contract-explicit and auditor-flagged — the stop sequence does
   not exist until 4.13, so 4.11 cannot wire into it. **Not a
   weakening; see NOTE-1.**
2. **Played/failed two-event semantics.** Contract §4 requires
   emission synchronously with `play()` ("no await between — the
   envelope's `monotonicMs` is the beep time"); contract §6 requires
   a `'failed'` event on `play()` rejection. The rejection is
   unknowable at call time, so the two requirements are jointly
   satisfiable only via two events: `'played'` = the attempt record
   (beep-time timestamp), `'failed'` = the outcome record (verbatim
   error, `detail: 'play-promise-rejected'`). The builder's
   resolution is the faithful one, documented as deviation #1.
   **Not invented semantics; see NOTE-2.**
3. **Visible flash interference.** Covered under AC2 — no credible
   interference path (200 ms, non-interactive, removed).
4. **Marker-never-fails-streams.** Traced: the start-marker block is
   inside try/catch; `getSyncMarker()` wiring-defect throw is
   caught; `emitStartMarker()` never throws outward (all internal
   paths guarded); zero-streams → no marker per contract.
5. **Event shape.** Six keys exactly, no `sequence` field — the
   contract's §5 is authoritative over the spawn brief's mention,
   and the standing rule (don't persist derivable values) supports
   log-sequence ordering for the stop double-beep.
6. **Source timestamps.** The envelope's `monotonicMs` +
   `clockSegmentId` are the only timestamps; no duplicates
   persisted (verified by the no-arithmetic scan).
7. **No manifest widening.** 16 keys confirmed.
8. **4.13 seam completeness.** `emitStopMarker()` takes no
   arguments (all deps injected at construction); exposed via
   `getSyncMarker` on the recorder handle (`recorder.js:1469`) and
   documented at both the definition and the call point. Complete
   for 4.13's use. **See NOTE-3.**
9. **No-AudioContext pin.** Genuine: the original 4.7 pin passes
   unmodified AND a new 8-file superset pin covers
   `sync_marker.js`. Playback is acoustic (speakers → room), not a
   mixer — the 4.7 prohibition is about routing audio *into* a
   `MediaStream`; playing a tone into the room is the documented
   opposite.
10. **Changed-files discipline.** Clean (AC8).
11. **V2 honesty.** The build report correctly labels what was and
    was not proven; no simulated-as-real claims.

## NOTEs (informational; none fails an AC)

- **NOTE-1 (auditor obligation): PLAN's stop marker is unwired.**
  The section-4 auditor must verify 4.13 calls
  `getSyncMarker().emitStopMarker()` before `recorder.stop()`.
  Until then, PLAN §4.11's "at session start and stop" is half
  satisfied by mechanism only. This is the contract's declared
  structure, not a 4.11 defect — but it is the single most
  important carry-forward from this task.
- **NOTE-2 (for 4.12's contract): played-vs-failed
  disambiguation.** A `'played'` audible event whose `markerId`
  also has a `'failed'` event means the beep never sounded; 4.12
  must not use the `'played'` timestamp as an alignment point in
  that case. The two-event semantics are recorded in DECISIONS.md
  ## 4.11, but 4.12's contract should state the rule explicitly.
- **NOTE-3 (for 4.13's contract): stop-beep audibility vs the
  onset-only rule.** Contract §4 says "no waiting for tone
  completion: alignment uses the marker ONSET; a truncated tail
  does not invalidate it." If 4.13 calls `recorder.stop()`
  synchronously after `emitStopMarker()`, the 250 ms beeps
  (150 ms apart) will be truncated in the recording to ~0 ms —
  yet AC11 requires the stop double-beep to be *distinguishable*
  in the recording on the owner device. 4.13's wiring should allow
  enough capture time for both beeps (or document why onset-only
  suffices for §7.9).
- **NOTE-4 (minor semantic polish):** when the `Audio`
  constructor is unavailable, `playBeep` emits `error:
  'audio-unavailable'` — a descriptive code, not a verbatim
  platform error. Honest and validator-legal (`string|null`),
  but the contract's "verbatim" rule contemplates platform
  errors; a future touch could move the code to `detail`. Not
  AC-failing.
- **NOTE-5 (documented deviation, informational):** `sessionId`
  rides the relay messages additively (offscreen→SW, SW→tab)
  beyond the contract §7 shapes. Additive, lenient-on-input on
  receipt, and required because the visible event's envelope
  needs a uuid `sessionId` pre-§5. No contract break.
- **NOTE-6 (4.12 alignment precision):** the visible event's
  timestamp marks fade-in *start* (overlay insertion), not full
  brightness (~60 ms later). Consistent with the audible
  onset rule; 4.12 should treat both modalities' marker
  timestamps as onset markers.
