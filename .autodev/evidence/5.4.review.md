The builder's "type normalization of valid input" reading is the faithful one; invalid non-blank input (`"12x"`) still passes through un-coerced → normalizer throws → honest `record-build-failed`. No SHOULD_FIX.
2. **playerColor detection honesty — verified against precedent.** The `flipped` class on `wc-chess-board` is a real, in-repo-used signal: `squareToXY` (chess_utils.js:248) inverts the coordinate mapping when `flipped` is present, i.e. "flipped ⇒ black at the bottom". The selector `document.querySelector("wc-chess-board")` is `getBoardElement` (chess_utils.js:353). The flipped→black / not-flipped→white mapping rests on the contract's documented assumption ("in Chess.com bot games the player sits at the bottom of the board") — an explicitly documented assumption, not fabricated evidence. AC11 (§7) will verify against the real page. No SHOULD_FIX.

No SHOULD_FIX.
