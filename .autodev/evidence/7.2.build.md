### Replay harness (test-only, never product code)

`replayFromFen(startFen, confirmedMoves)` — applies `{from, to, promotion}`
moves to a starting FEN via the vendored chess.js, returning the FEN after
each ply. Per 7.13's rule ("a collection correctness check, not a shipped
analysis command"), this harness lives in the test file only.

### AC1 — promotion

`e7e8` with `promotion: 'q'` → `4Q2k/...` (queen on e8). Underpromotion
`b7b8n` → knight on b8.

### AC2/AC3 — castling

`e1g1` → `R...RK1`, rights `kq`. `e1c1` → `2KR3R`, rights `kq`.

### AC4 — en passant

`e5xd6` e.p. (from `...3pP3... w - d6`) → `3P4/...` — black pawn removed
from d5 (not d6), white pawn on d6.

### AC5 — full-game replay

Two chains: (1) 7-ply Italian game with kingside castling, asserting the
final FEN; (2) promotion + en passant chain (`d7d5`, `exd6 e.p.`, `d6d7`,
`d8=Q`), asserting the e.p. intermediate FEN and the final queen-on-d8 FEN.

### AC6 — promotion vocabulary

Pinned: `move_confirmed.promotion` is lowercase `'q' | 'r' | 'b' | 'n'`
(game_records.js `requirePromotion`); chess.js accepts all four in the
move object. `promotion: null` on a promotion move is rejected by
chess.js (returns null) — the collection must record the piece
(3.1.7), never null.
