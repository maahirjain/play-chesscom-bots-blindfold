## Deviations from the contract

None in exported behavior. Two judgment calls made within contract latitude:

1. **`createInitialConditions` rejects unexpected extra keys** (`TypeError`).
   The contract says "requires all seven fields present"; AC4 requires the
   record to contain exactly seven keys. Rejecting extras keeps the stored
   record's exact key set and surfaces caller mistakes early instead of
   silently dropping fields.
2. **`normalizeAssistanceSettings` rejects non-finite numbers** (`NaN`,
   `Infinity`) with `TypeError`. The contract lists `number` as an allowed
   value type, but non-finite numbers are not plain-JSON values and would
   corrupt the AC21 JSON-safety invariant; the stricter reading was chosen
   (strictness is not weakening).
